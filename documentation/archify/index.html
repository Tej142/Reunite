Campus Desk Claim & 2FA OTP Custody Lifecycle

The terminal operational lifecycle governing item custody and handover. Spans candidate match alerts, claimant proof submission, campus desk verification, 6-digit HMAC-SHA256 OTP verification, and secure physical return (or terminal rejection/lockout).

Entry State: Matched item generates automated alert and claim invitation
Proof Submission: Claimant provides serial numbers, receipts, and photos
Cryptographic 2FA: 6-digit HMAC-SHA256 OTP valid for 5 minutes with 5 max attempts
Terminal States: Reunited (custody closed), Expired (retry), or Rejected (fraud)
